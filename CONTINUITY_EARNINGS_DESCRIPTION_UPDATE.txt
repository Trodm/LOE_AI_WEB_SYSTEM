V5 Continuity Update

The earnings-description engine has been updated to enforce medico-legal actuarial chronology:

PRE-ACCIDENT:
1. Earnings/employment status at the time of accident must appear first.
2. Subsequent employment changes follow in date order.
3. Promotions/progression follow in date/age order.
4. Alternative/future projected progression follows.
5. Retirement/salary inflation sentence is always the final bullet.

POST-ACCIDENT:
1. Immediate post-accident employment/income impact appears first.
2. Recuperation/unemployment/no-income periods follow.
3. Return-to-work/current earnings follow.
4. Reduced/alternative/future progression follows.
5. Retirement/nil-to-retirement sentence is always the final bullet.

This update affects ai_extractor.py and all downloaded earnings descriptions generated through compose_earnings_description().
